---
name: Warm
description: Personal and friendly. A little about why the work matters to the candidate. Good for startups, nonprofits and design.
---
Write like someone who cares about the work and is glad to talk about it. Let one sentence say why the work matters to
the candidate, drawn from the CV or the answers. Use "I" freely and speak to the reader as a person. Stay specific: warmth
comes from real details, not from enthusiasm words.
