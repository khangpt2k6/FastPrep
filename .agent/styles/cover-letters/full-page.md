{{why_company: 80 to 100 words. What the candidate learned about this company and why it matters to them. {{story: 140 to 170 words. The piece of work closest to this role, told as a story: what was asked, what the real problem turned out to be, the first attempt that fell short if there was one, what the candidate changed, and the result with its one signature number. The last one or two sentences say what the candidate learned.}}

{{second_story: 90 to 110 words. A second piece of work from another side of the candidate (leading, teaching, working with people), opened by linking it to the lesson of the first. One signature number. End on the habit or question the candidate carries from it, and tie that to this role's work in one sentence.}}

{{shared_belief: 65 to 80 words. Come back to the company: one product, service or principle of theirs by name, and the belief of the candidate's that matches it, shown by how they actually work.}}

{{closing: 30 to 40 words. What the candidate would bring to the team and to the people the company serves, said with humility. Thank them, and ask for a conversation.}}
