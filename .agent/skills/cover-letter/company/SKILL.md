---
name: Why this company
description: Say why this role at this company, using only what the posting says, never generic praise.
order: 3
---
The reader wants to know why this candidate wants this job, not any job.

- Point at something specific in the posting: the product, the problem the team works on, the users, a technology, how
  the team works. Name it.
- Tie it to the candidate: a project, a course, a past role or an interest from the CV or the answers that connects to it.
- When the posting says little about the company, talk about the work the role does instead. That is still specific.
- No empty praise: "industry leader", "innovative company", "great culture", "amazing mission" say nothing.
- Do not flatter the reader or the company. Plain interest reads as more sincere than excitement.
