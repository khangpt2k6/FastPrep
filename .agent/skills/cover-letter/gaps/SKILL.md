---
name: "Strength-Based Positioning"
description: "Emphasize demonstrated capabilities and transferable experience without drawing unnecessary attention to unmet requirements."
order: 6
---
Almost no candidate meets every line of a posting.

- Never mention a requirement the candidate does not meet. No "although I lack", "while I have not yet", "I may not have".
- Do not explain gaps in employment, career changes or grades unless the answers ask for it. Let the CV speak for dates.
- A junior candidate leads with projects, coursework, internships and how fast they learned something real. Name the
  thing they learned and what they did with it.
- A career changer leads with the skills that carry over, shown in the work they did, not with the change itself.
