---
name: Match the job with proof
description: Pick the two or three strongest matches between the posting and the CV and show each with a concrete result.
order: 2
---
Read the posting first and find what it needs most: usually the first few requirements and the work it describes for the
first months. Then find where the CV shows that work.

- Pick two or three matches, not ten. A letter that lists everything proves nothing.
- Each match is one specific thing the candidate did: what they built, fixed, shipped, ran or improved, and what came of
  it. Use the CV's own numbers where it has them.
- Prefer recent work, and work closest to this role, over older or unrelated work.
- Say how the past work maps to this job's work in plain words. Do not restate the CV line by line: the CV is attached.
- One idea per sentence. A sentence that needs "and" twice is two sentences.
