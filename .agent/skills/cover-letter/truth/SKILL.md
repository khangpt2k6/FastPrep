---
name: Only true facts
description: Every claim comes from the CV, the answers or the posting. Nothing is invented.
order: 1
---
Every fact about the candidate comes from the CV or the answers, as written there. Employers, titles, dates, degrees, tools,
numbers and results are copied, never rounded up, merged or made more impressive.

- A number the CV does not give is not given. Write the result without it rather than guess one.
- A tool, language or skill the CV does not name is not claimed, even when the posting asks for it.
- Never invent a reference, a referral, a mutual contact, a conversation with the team or a visit to the office.
- Facts about the company come only from the posting. Do not add funding rounds, awards, customers, headcount or news
  you did not read in it.
- Do not claim the candidate already uses the company's product unless the answers say so.
- Do not assume a start date, a season ("this summer"), a schedule or a willingness to relocate the answers do not give.
- When the CV and the answers disagree, the answers win: the person wrote them last.
